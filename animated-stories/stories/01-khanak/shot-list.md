# खनक · KHANAK: shot list and script
31 shots · 2:18 · 3,312 frames at 24 fps · 9:16 (1080×1920)
Frame numbers start at 1. Use them as timeline markers in Blender and bind each camera to its marker (select the camera, then Ctrl+B in the timeline).

**Camera shorthand:** ECU extreme close-up · CU close-up · MCU medium close-up · MS medium · WS wide · OTS over the shoulder · POV point of view.
**Lens guide:** 24mm for room wides, 35–50mm for most character shots, 85mm for emotional close-ups. Keep the camera at Chhotu's eye height (about 1 m) in his scenes, so the world feels like his.
**Vertical framing:** keep faces in the upper third. Subtitles go just under the centre of the frame, and the bottom 20% stays clear because the Shorts/Reels interface covers it.
