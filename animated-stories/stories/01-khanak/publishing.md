## 6. Where and when
| Platform | Version | Why |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube Shorts | HI audio + EN subs | Main discovery |
| Instagram Reels | HI audio + EN subs | Your existing audience. Cross-post to Facebook |
| Facebook Reels | HI audio + EN subs | Older viewers, who share emotional family stories a lot ⚠ (observation) |
| Second upload a few days later | AS audio + EN subs | For your Assam audience. Don't post both on the same day |
| YouTube long-form (optional) | Same file | It can be added to playlists and show up in suggested videos |

- Post when your own reel analytics show your audience is most active. Check Insights and YouTube Studio rather than guessing.
- **Teaser:** 2–3 days before release, post S01–S02 (the smash and the frozen note) as a 6-second Short with "पर्ची किसकी है? 👀 Full story on [date]".
- **Behind the scenes:** a timelapse of modelling Dadi, or of recording your real dadi's voice. Creator content like this often grows a channel by itself.

## 7. What to measure after 7 days
| Metric | Where | Good sign |
|---|---|---|
| Viewed vs. swiped away (Shorts) | YouTube Studio | Most viewers stay past the hook |
| Average % viewed | YouTube Studio / IG Insights | Above 70% for a 2-minute Short is strong ⚠ (rule of thumb) |
| Retention dip | Retention graph | Find where people leave and tighten that shot in the next film |
| Shares and saves | Both | Shares matter more than likes for this story |
| Comments that tag someone | Comments | Your end card worked |
