# Animated stories: short Blender films for India
Short 3D animated story films (1:30–2:30, vertical 9:16), made solo in Blender, in Hindi with Assamese and English versions.
Project rules: `CLAUDE.md` in this folder.

## Start here
1. `research/audience-research.md`: what Indian audiences love in animation, with data and sources.
2. `research/story-ideas.md`: 12 original story ideas, scored, with a recommended order.
3. `stories/01-khanak/`: the complete package for the first film.

## Workflow for every film
1. Story bible: one want, one sacrifice, one payoff.
2. Shot list with frame numbers.
3. Scratch voice (Google AI Studio), then an animatic in Grease Pencil.
4. Test the animatic on 5 people without explaining anything. Fix the story.
5. Build or reuse characters, sets and props, then rig.
6. Layout, then animation (emotional shots first), then FX.
7. Light and render (EEVEE, PNG sequence).
8. Record the real voices, mix, add subtitles, and export the HI, AS and EN versions.
9. Teaser, release, and check the analytics after 7 days.
