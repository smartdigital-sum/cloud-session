- **hook**: Full-bleed cinematic shot, camera push, flash/shake on impact, big kinetic title.

- **split**: Two-panel comparison with zig-zag divider; light moves to the side being talked about.
- **bar**: Animated bar chart with count-up values and a callout card.

- **pyramid**: Three-tier pyramid built block by block with icons and labels.
- **flow**: Boxes connected by arrows that light up in order (cause and effect / process).

- **quote**: Portrait medallion + book cover + key idea typed on a paper card.

- **symbols**: Grid of object cut-outs popping in, each with a one-line meaning.
- **counter**: Big number counting up/down with a gauge or meter.
- **concept**: Exam keyword card: term, definition, small icon, 'EXAM KEYWORD' tag.
- **quiz**: Question card with a countdown ring; viewers answer in comments.
- **outro**: Like / subscribe / next episode card with characters waving.
