# 03 · Reel: A Random Number Changed Your Answer

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a claim with nothing to wait for. | **Lock-in** teases that it even works on judges. |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("even experts?", "so what's going on?"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = set your own number first; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |

## Scene table

| # | Part | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:04 | Game-show wheel clicks to 10; a ghostly 10 lingers | **A RANDOM NUMBER CHANGED YOUR ANSWER** | A random number just changed your answer. And you didn't notice. |
| 2 | Lock-in | 0:04–0:07 | A judge's gavel hovers | IT EVEN WORKS ON JUDGES | It even works on judges. |
| 3 | Body 1 | 0:07–0:18 | Wheel stops on 10 or 65; two guess bars: ~25% vs ~45% | 10 → 25% · 65 → 45% | In 1974, a wheel rigged to stop on 10 or 65 shifted people's guesses about Africa's share of UN countries: about 25 versus 45 percent. |
| 4 | Rehook 1 | 0:18–0:20 | Gavel comes down | EXPERTS? | And experts? |
| 5 | Body 2 | 0:20–0:27 | Judges roll loaded dice; sentence bars grow | EVEN JUDGES | Experienced judges rolled loaded dice before sentencing. Higher numbers, longer sentences. |

| 7 | Body 3 | 0:29–0:40 | MRP crossed out; first offers; a stack of lab reports with a green check | THIS IS ANCHORING ⚓ · IT HOLDS UP | That's anchoring: the first number pulls every number after it. MRPs, first offers, crossed-out prices. And it held up when re-tested across dozens of labs. |
