# 05 · Reel: Same Reading. Everyone.

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~110 English (~135 wpm with pauses)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start. Respectful of beliefs: the point is a perception effect, not mocking astrology.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | A typed "personal" card slides toward the viewer | **SAME READING. EVERYONE.** | This "personal" reading fit everyone in the class. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:13 | 1940s classroom; rating cards go up; tally settles at 4.26 / 5 | 1948 · RATED 4.3 / 5 | In 1948, Bertram Forer gave each student a "personal" personality description. They rated it about 4.3 out of 5. |
| 3 | 0:13–0:21 | Envelopes fanned out: the same page inside each | EVERYONE GOT THE SAME TEXT | Then he revealed: every student got the exact same text, mostly built from astrology-book lines. |
| 4 | 0:21–0:30 | A coin: OUTGOING / RESERVED; a card "You have unused potential" | FITS ALMOST ANYONE | Lines like "You have unused potential" or "Sometimes outgoing, sometimes reserved" fit almost anyone. |
| 5 | 0:30–0:40 | Circus poster "BARNUM"; a blank outline filled with the viewer's memories | THE BARNUM EFFECT 🎪 | It's called the Barnum effect. Flattering, two-sided statements, and we fill in the rest from our own memories. |
| 6 | 0:40–0:51 | The card held up next to three friends, all nodding "me too!" | THE FRIEND TEST | Quick test for any reading or quiz: would this fit my friend too? If yes, it's describing humans, not you. |
| 7 | 0:51–0:57 | Phone with a quiz result "This is SO me" | MOST ACCURATE QUIZ? 👇 | What's the most "scarily accurate" quiz you've taken? Tell me below. |

**Loop:** scene 7's quiz result cuts straight back to the "personal" card in scene 1.
