**Text**

```
[knowingly] Why do you finish movies you hate?
[curious] Because you already paid. It's called the sunk cost fallacy: money or time that's gone, steering what you do next.
[intrigued] In a 1982 theatre study, people randomly charged full price for season tickets went to more plays than people given discounts.
[playfully] Quitting turns a cost into a loss, and losses feel louder. Hello, "paisa vasool" buffet plate.
[serious] Even governments fall for it. It's also called the Concorde fallacy, after the supersonic jet.
[warmly] The fix: ask, "If I started fresh today, would I choose this?" If not, leaving doesn't waste it. Staying adds to it.
[friendly] What did you finish only because you'd started? Tell me below.
```
