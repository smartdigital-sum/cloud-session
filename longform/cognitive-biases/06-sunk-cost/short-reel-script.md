# 06 · Reel: Why Do You Stay?

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a question with nothing to wait for. | **Lock-in** teases that it fools governments too, and one question breaks it. |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("scientists caught it in a real theatre", "why can't we let go?"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = the fresh-start question; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |

## Scene table

| # | Part | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:04 | Dark cinema; a slumped figure eyes the EXIT, then a ticket stub | **WHY DO YOU STAY?** | Why do you finish movies you hate? |
| 2 | Lock-in | 0:04–0:07 | A white jet flashes past; a sealed card: THE QUESTION | IT FOOLS GOVERNMENTS TOO | The answer fools governments too. And one question breaks it. |
| 3 | Body 1 | 0:07–0:13 | A backpack labelled ALREADY SPENT tilts the figure forward | SUNK COST FALLACY | Because you already paid. It's called the sunk cost fallacy: money or time that's gone, steering what you do next. |
| 4 | Rehook 1 | 0:13–0:15 | A theatre marquee lights up | CAUGHT IN A REAL THEATRE | Scientists caught it in a real theatre. |
| 5 | Body 2 | 0:15–0:25 | Full-price ticket holders attend more plays than discount holders | 1982 THEATRE TEST 🎭 | In 1982, people randomly charged full price for season tickets went to more plays than people given discounts. |
| 6 | Rehook 2 | 0:25–0:27 | A hand grips an empty plate | WHY CAN'T WE LET GO? | Why can't we let go? |
| 7 | Body 3 | 0:27–0:39 | "Paisa vasool" buffet plate; then a supersonic jet | "PAISA VASOOL" 🍛 · CONCORDE ✈️ | Quitting turns a cost into a loss, and losses feel louder. Hello, "paisa vasool" buffet plate. Even governments fall for it. It's also called the Concorde fallacy. |
| 8 | Closing | 0:39–0:51 | A fork in the road: "START FRESH TODAY?" | THE FRESH-START QUESTION | The fix: ask, "If I started fresh today, would I choose this?" If not, leaving doesn't waste it. Staying adds to it. |
| 9 | CTA | 0:51–0:57 | Ticket stub; cut to scene 1 | WHAT DID YOU FINISH? 👇 | Full video's linked below. What did you finish only because you'd started? |

**Loop:** scene 9's ticket stub cuts straight back to the cinema in scene 1.
