# 02 · Reel: ₹499 = ₹500?

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a claim with nothing to wait for. | **Lock-in** teases "even car dealers fall for it". |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("it gets stranger", "even professionals"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = round up; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |

## Scene table

| # | Part | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:04 | Two price tags: ₹499 and ₹500; a scale tips to ₹500 | **₹499 = ₹500?** | ₹499 feels way cheaper than ₹500. And that's no accident. |
| 2 | Lock-in | 0:04–0:07 | A suit-wearing silhouette with a "?" | EVEN EXPERTS FALL FOR IT | It even fools professionals. |
| 3 | Body 1 | 0:07–0:16 | Eyes scan left to right; the first digit glows | THE FIRST DIGIT WINS | Your brain reads left to right, and the first digit gets most of the weight. ₹499 gets filed under "four hundred". |

| 5 | Body 2 | 0:18–0:27 | $39 tag outsells a $34 tag | $39 BEAT $34 | In catalogue tests, one dress sold more at $39 than at a cheaper $34. |

| 7 | Body 3 | 0:29–0:40 | Odometer crosses 10,000-mile marks; price drops each time | EVEN CAR DEALERS | Used-car prices dropped every time the odometer crossed a 10,000-mile mark. Though luxury brands often skip nines, because nines can signal "cheap". |
