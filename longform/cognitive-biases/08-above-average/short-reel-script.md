# 08 · Reel: Are You Above Average?

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a question with nothing to wait for. | **Lock-in** teases that the maths makes it impossible. |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("here's the catch", "and it's not just 1981"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = feeling → measure; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |

## Scene table

| # | Part | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:04 | Thought bubbles over every vehicle: "I'm a good driver." | **ARE YOU ABOVE AVERAGE?** | Are you an above-average driver? So is almost everyone. |
| 2 | Lock-in | 0:04–0:07 | A calculator flashes ERROR | THE MATHS SAYS NO | And the maths says that's impossible. |
| 3 | Body 1 | 0:07–0:15 | Two bars: 93% (US) and 69% (Sweden) | 93% · 69% · 1981 | In 1981, 93% of US students said they drove better than the median. In Sweden, 69%. |
| 4 | Rehook 1 | 0:15–0:17 | A ruler drops onto the bars | THE CATCH | Here's the catch. |
| 5 | Body 2 | 0:17–0:24 | A line splits a crowd exactly in half | ONLY HALF CAN BE | Only half of any group can be above the median. That's what median means. |
| 6 | Rehook 2 | 0:24–0:26 | Calendar flips to 2023 | NOT JUST 1981 | And it's not just 1981. |
| 7 | Body 3 | 0:26–0:42 | 1,200+ figures; a window into one head shows good intentions, others show only actions | STILL TRUE IN 2023 · YOU SEE YOUR REASONS | A 2023 repeat with 1,200+ people found the same. It's called the better-than-average effect. Why? You see your own intentions, but only other people's actions. And most of us think we're less biased than average too. |
| 8 | Closing | 0:42–0:51 | A gut-feeling icon swaps for a measuring tape | FEELING → MEASURE | The fix: for decisions that matter, swap the feeling for a measure. Ask, "How would I know?" |
| 9 | CTA | 0:51–0:57 | Traffic bubbles; cut to scene 1 | SURE YOU WERE GREAT AT…? 👇 | Full video's linked below. What were you sure you were great at, until you checked? |

**Loop:** scene 9's traffic bubbles cut straight back to scene 1.
