# 08 · Reel: Are You Above Average?

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~110 English (~135 wpm with pauses)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Traffic; every vehicle has a bubble "I'm a good driver" | **ARE YOU ABOVE AVERAGE?** | Are you an above-average driver? So is almost everyone. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:12 | Two rooms: 93 of 100 hands up (US), 69 of 100 (Sweden) | 93% · 69% · 1981 | In 1981, 93% of US students said they drove better than the median. In Sweden, 69%. |
| 3 | 0:12–0:21 | 100 figures in a line; MEDIAN marker in the middle; only 50 fit to its right | ONLY HALF CAN BE | But only half of any group can be above the median. That's what median means. |
| 4 | 0:21–0:30 | 1,203 dots, mostly on the "above" side | STILL TRUE IN 2023 | A 2023 repeat with 1,200+ people found the same. It's called the better-than-average effect. |
| 5 | 0:30–0:42 | Inside your car: a reason bubble; outside: a frowning stranger. A magnifying glass spots "BIAS!" in others | YOU SEE YOUR REASONS | Why? You see your own intentions, but only other people's actions. And most of us think we're less biased than average too. |
| 6 | 0:42–0:51 | A CONFIDENCE fuel gauge + a DATA map | FEELING → MEASURE | The fix: for decisions that matter, swap the feeling for a measure. Ask, "How would I know?" |
| 7 | 0:51–0:57 | A "TOP 10%" badge being peeled off | SURE YOU WERE GREAT AT…? 👇 | What were you sure you were great at, until you checked? Tell me below. |

**Loop:** scene 7's peeled badge cuts straight back to the traffic bubbles in scene 1.
