[thoughtful] And who pays for that relief? Future you.

[short pause] A stranger who happens to be you.

[serious] That loan comes with interest.

[warmly] So the cure isn't more discipline. It's two small, kind moves.

You don't need to feel ready to start. [long pause] Very often, the feeling comes after you start.

[gently] So next time you're avoiding something, don't ask, "Why am I so lazy?"
