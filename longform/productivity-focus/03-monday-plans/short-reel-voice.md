[calm] Your Monday plan was never possible.
[playfully] Monday you plans the gym, ten tasks, twenty pages. Wednesday you… what plan? That's not weakness. It's a bias.
[thoughtful] Kahneman and Tversky called it the planning fallacy. In one study, students expected their thesis to take thirty-four days. On average, it took fifty-six.
[serious] Even experts fall for it. The Sydney Opera House was planned for four years. It took fourteen, at about fourteen times the cost.
[gently] Why? We plan the work, not the interruptions. Monday's plan has no room for real life.

[friendly] How many things were on your Monday list… and how many are done? Tell me below.
