# 04 · Reel: You Can't Multitask

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~55–60s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook had a claim but no stakes, and nothing to wait for. | **Hook** adds "it's costing you"; **lock-in** teases the clown and the rare group who can. |
| Studies ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("now put that behind the wheel", "so can anyone do it?"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = three fixes; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |

## Scene table

| # | Part | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook | 0:00–0:04 | Phone, snack, laptop; an ATTENTION spotlight jumps between them | **YOU CAN'T MULTITASK** | You can't multitask. Not really. And every time you try, it's costing you. |
| 2 | Lock-in | 0:04–0:08 | A tiny unicycle wheel rolls past; a gold cape flutters | A CLOWN PROVED IT 🤡 | It took a clown on a unicycle to prove it. And a tiny group of people who break the rule. |
| 3 | Body 1 | 0:08–0:20 | Track switch in a brain: TASK A stops, TASK B starts; clown crosses a campus, a walker on a call walks right past | 1 IN 4 NOTICED | Your brain doesn't do two thinking tasks at once. It switches. In one study, a clown rode a unicycle across a campus. Of people on their phones, only about one in four noticed. |
| 4 | Rehook 1 | 0:20–0:22 | Campus slides away; a steering wheel | BEHIND THE WHEEL? | Now put that behind the wheel. |
| 5 | Body 2 | 0:22–0:31 | Simulator; slow reaction bar; earpiece icon ≈ wine glass "0.08% US LIMIT" | HANDS-FREE ≠ BRAIN-FREE | In a driving simulator, phone talkers, even hands-free, were as impaired as drunk drivers at the US legal limit. |
| 6 | Rehook 2 | 0:31–0:33 | Hundreds of tiny figures; a few glow | CAN ANYONE DO IT? | So can anyone do it? |
| 7 | Body 3 | 0:33–0:42 | 200 tiny drivers flash red; 5 glow gold with capes | SUPERTASKERS ≈ 2.5% | About two and a half percent of people tested. "Supertaskers." The rest of us? No. |
| 8 | Closing | 0:42–0:51 | Cards flip ONE THING · BATCH · OUT OF REACH; phone slides into a drawer | ONE THING AT A TIME | So do one thing at a time. Batch your messages. Put the phone out of reach. |
| 9 | CTA | 0:51–0:57 | Browser bar with a row of tiny tabs; cut to scene 1 | HOW MANY TABS? 👀 | Full video's linked below. So, how many tabs do you have open right now? |

**Loop:** scene 9's crowded tabs cut straight back to the jumping spotlight in scene 1.
