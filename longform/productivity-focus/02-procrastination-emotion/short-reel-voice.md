## English

**Style instructions**
```
You are a calm, deep-voiced, wise mentor narrating a 60-second vertical reel called "You're not lazy, you're avoiding a feeling". Speak directly to one listener ("you"), like a trusted older friend. Indian English accent. About 140 words per minute, with short deliberate pauses between lines. **Text**
```
```
[firmly] You're not lazy. You're avoiding a feeling, and that's why willpower keeps failing.
[intrigued] And the way most of us fight it? It makes it worse.
[knowingly] You'll clean your room, reply to old messages… anything but the task. Researchers call it short-term mood repair. The task brings boredom or self-doubt, so you switch tabs. Instant relief.
[knowingly] But that relief is a loan.
[serious] In a 1997 study, student procrastinators felt great early on… then ended the term more stressed, sicker, with lower grades.
[leaning in] Now, that usual fix?
[gently] Beating yourself up. In a 2010 study, students who forgave themselves after procrastinating on one exam procrastinated less on the next.
[warmly] So forgive yesterday. Then shrink the task: not "write the report", just "open the file".
[friendly] Full video's linked below. So, what are you avoiding right now?
```
