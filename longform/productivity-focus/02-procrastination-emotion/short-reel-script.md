# 02 · Reel: You're Not Lazy. You're Avoiding a Feeling.

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~60s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Opened on the answer ("avoiding a feeling") with nothing left to wonder about. | **Hook** keeps the claim, adds stakes ("why willpower keeps failing"); **lock-in** teases that the usual fix backfires. |
| Scenes ran as a list of studies. | Two **rehooks** ("that relief is a loan", "remember that usual fix?") reset attention. |
| Ended with advice + question. | **Closing** pays off with the two moves; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |
