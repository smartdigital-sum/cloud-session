# 02 · Reel: You're Not Lazy. You're Avoiding a Feeling.

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~60s · **Words:** ~135 English (calm mentor pace, ~140 wpm)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Figure frozen mid-scrub in a spotless room; an untouched laptop blinks in the corner | **YOU'RE NOT LAZY.** | You're not lazy. You're avoiding a *feeling*. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:12 | Fast montage: cleaning, replying to old chats, rearranging app icons; the laptop stays shut | ANYTHING BUT THE TASK | You'll clean your room, reply to old messages, reorganise your phone… anything except the one task. Lazy people don't work that hard. |
| 3 | 0:12–0:22 | Laptop opens; grey clouds (BORED, "WHAT IF I FAIL?") drift out; click to a new tab → clouds vanish, small sun | SHORT-TERM MOOD REPAIR · _Sirois & Pychyl_ | Researchers call it short-term mood repair. The task brings boredom or self-doubt. You switch tabs. Instant relief. |
| 4 | 0:22–0:32 | Semester timeline: hammock early → thermometer, red grade at the end; receipt prints "PAID WITH INTEREST" | FUTURE YOU PAYS · 1997 | But future you pays. In a 1997 study, student procrastinators felt great early on… then ended the term more stressed, sicker, with lower grades. |
| 5 | 0:32–0:42 | GUILT cloud joins the loop and it spins faster; a sticky note "I forgive myself" shrinks the next storm | GUILT FEEDS IT · 2010 | And guilt makes it worse. In a 2010 study, students who forgave themselves after procrastinating on one exam procrastinated less on the next. |
| 6 | 0:42–0:51 | Cards flip 1 FORGIVE · 2 SHRINK; "WRITE THE REPORT" boulder shrinks to an "OPEN THE FILE" pebble | FORGIVE → SHRINK IT | So forgive yesterday. Then shrink the task: not "write the report", just "open the file". |
| 7 | 0:51–1:00 | Text swap ~~WHY AM I LAZY?~~ → WHAT AM I FEELING?; cut back to the room from scene 1 | WHAT ARE YOU AVOIDING? 👇 | Next time, don't ask "Why am I lazy?" Ask "What am I feeling?" What task are you avoiding right now? Tell me below. |

**Loop:** scene 7 ends on "What am I feeling?" in the same room as scene 1, which flows straight back into "You're not lazy. You're avoiding a feeling."
