## HOOK (0:00–0:10)

**[NARRATION]**
Your attention on a screen has dropped to under a minute. And you probably haven't noticed.
In the early 2000s, office workers stayed on one screen for about two and a half minutes before switching. In more recent studies? (pause) About forty-seven seconds.
Your focus isn't gone. It's being taken. Quietly.

**[VISUAL]** Frame 1 is already moving: a stopwatch on a computer screen counts down fast from 2:30 and slams to **0:47**. Office worker silhouettes flick between windows faster and faster. The cursor drifts away on its own.

_Tone: firm on line one; clear numbers; quiet "Quietly."_

## LOCK-IN (0:10–0:40)

**[NARRATION]**
So let's do a self-check. Seven silent signs your focus is being hijacked. Keep count as we go.
The first few are easy to spot. (pause) The last one, most people miss in themselves.
And at the end, every sign gets its fix… and I'll ask for your number.

**[VISUAL]** A clipboard slides in with seven empty boxes and a tally counter at 0. Box 7 is drawn faintly, almost invisible. A ring of seven small keys glints at the bottom edge.

_Tone: warm, inviting "Keep count"; intrigued on "most people miss in themselves"._

## MAIN BODY (SEVEN SIGNS)

### BODY 1: Sign 1, Screen jumping (0:40–1:00)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign one: you jump between screens before you even notice.
Attention researcher Gloria Mark found that forty-seven-second average by tracking people's screens at work, across years of studies.
And every jump costs a little time to restart. That's the switch cost from our multitasking video.

**[VISUAL]** A cursor darts between tabs like a pinball; each bounce leaves a tiny "+0.x s". Caption: _Gloria Mark, UC Irvine_. The track-switch icon from video 04 clunks. Tally counter: box 1.

_Tone: clear, curious._

### BODY 2: Sign 2, The phone reach (1:00–1:20)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign two: your hand reaches for your phone before you've decided to.
Or you feel it buzz, and it didn't.
That's a mind on standby, always waiting for the next alert. And we saw how even a notification you ignore can cost you.

**[VISUAL]** A hand drifts toward a phone on its own, like a puppet on a string. A figure pats an empty pocket; "?" pops up. The radar dish from video 05 sweeps on the head: STANDBY. Box 2.

_Tone: knowing, lightly amused._

### BODY 3: Sign 3, Night-time replays (1:20–1:40)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign three: unfinished tasks replay in your head at night.
That's an open loop. In our very first video, we saw that writing a specific plan, what, when and where, can quiet it.

**[VISUAL]** The dark bedroom and orbiting sticky notes from video 01. A notebook opens: WHAT · WHEN · WHERE; one loop closes with a soft click. Box 3.

_Tone: gentle, hushed._

### REHOOK 1 (1:40)

**[NARRATION]**
Those first three, you can see coming. (pause) The next ones hide inside a busy day.

**[VISUAL]** The tally counter clicks to 3; the scene fills with a busy to-do montage.

_Tone: a lift; leaning in._

### BODY 4: Sign 4, Busy, but stuck (1:40–2:00)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign four: you're busy all day, but the important thing didn't move.
You cleaned, replied, organised… everything except the one task.
That's not laziness. It's avoiding a feeling. Shrink the task until the feeling shrinks too.

**[VISUAL]** The fast cleaning montage from video 02; the laptop stays shut in the corner. A "WRITE THE REPORT" boulder shrinks to an "OPEN THE FILE" pebble. Box 4.

_Tone: knowing, kind; no judgement._

### BODY 5: Sign 5, Wednesday collapse (2:00–2:20)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign five: your plans keep collapsing by Wednesday.
That's the planning fallacy. You planned a perfect week, with no room for real life. Plan for Wednesday you, and leave empty space.

**[VISUAL]** The torn planner from video 03; interruptions fly in like birds. It rebuilds with white gaps that absorb them. Box 5.

_Tone: thoughtful, practical._

### BODY 6: Sign 6, No empty moments (2:20–2:40)

**[NARRATION]**
Sign six: you can't wait two minutes without your phone.
A queue. A lift. A kettle. Every empty moment, filled.
Those gaps are where your mind used to rest and connect ideas. Give a few of them back.

**[VISUAL]** Quick cuts from video 08: queue, lift, kettle, each with a phone rising into frame. Then one gap stays empty: a person looking out of a window as a small idea glows. Box 6.

_Tone: warm, slightly wistful._

### REHOOK 2 (2:40)

**[NARRATION]**
And the last sign is the hardest to count… because it feels like nothing's wrong.

**[VISUAL]** Box 7 on the clipboard fades into view; the tally counter hesitates.

_Tone: quiet, serious._

### BODY 7: Sign 7, Tired but "fine" (2:40–3:05)

**[NARRATION]**
And sign seven, the quietest one: you're tired, but you tell yourself you're fine.
Remember the study where people slept six hours a night for two weeks? Their attention kept slipping, and they barely noticed.
If you feel fine on too little sleep… (pause) that may be the sign itself.

**[VISUAL]** The 14-night calendar from video 09; the attention meter sinks while a thought bubble says "I'm fine 👍". The meter blinks red. Box 7 is the last to fill; the tally counter pulses.

_Tone: serious, slow; the most weight in the video._

## CLOSING (3:05–3:30)

_Pays off the hook and the lock-in promise._

**[NARRATION]**
So, what's your number? There's no official score here. It's a mirror, not a test. But if you counted five or more, your focus is being pulled hard.
Here's the good news: every sign has a fix, and you've already seen them all. Close loops with a plan. Shrink the task. Plan for real life. One thing at a time. Phone in another room. Protect your best hours. Rest, and sleep.
(pause) Your focus was never broken. It just needs protecting.

**[VISUAL]** The clipboard's seven ticked boxes each turn into a small key. The keys fly to the nine-video grid, each unlocking its matching thumbnail (01, 02, 03, 04, 05, 07, 08/09, with the tomato timer from 06 on "protect your best hours"). Final frame: the worker from the hook, calm, the glowing thread back in place and steady.

_Tone: warm certainty (peak); rhythmic list of fixes; slow, gentle last line._
