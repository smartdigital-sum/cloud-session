# 10 · Reel: 7 Signs Your Focus Is Hijacked

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. Signs are grouped two per scene to fit 60s. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a list title with no stakes. | **Hook** adds "you probably haven't noticed"; **lock-in** teases sign 7. |
| Seven signs in a row, no turns. | Two **rehooks** (before signs 4 and 7). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = every sign has a fix; **CTA** = full video + score question that loops to scene 1. |
