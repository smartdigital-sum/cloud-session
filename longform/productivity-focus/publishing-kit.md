# Publishing kit: Productivity & Focus (10 videos + 10 reels)

Everything you need to upload the series: language plan, schedule, playlist text, and per-video titles, descriptions, tags, pinned comments, end screens, cards and reel captions. Scripts and voice prompts are in the numbered folders; the index is in [README.md](README.md).

---

## 1. Before you upload anything

- [ ] Clear the open fact checks in [README.md → Before production](README.md#before-production-open-checks) (videos 01, 03, 05, 07, 08).
- [ ] Native-speaker review of the Hindi and Assamese voice files; test Assamese TTS on each hook first.
- [ ] **Re-time the chapters after recording.** The chapter lists below are script estimates. YouTube needs: first chapter at `0:00`, at least 3 chapters, each at least 10 seconds. Hindi/Assamese tracks run 5–10% longer, so each language needs its own timings if it is uploaded as a separate video.
- [ ] **AI voice disclosure.** YouTube asks creators to mark realistic altered or synthetic content in YouTube Studio when uploading. A text-to-speech narrator reading your own script is usually treated as production help rather than misleading content, but ⚠ check the current wording of the "Altered content" question in Studio. Either way, the description footer below says the narration is AI-voiced, which is the honest default.
- [ ] Thumbnails: use the thumbnail text from each section, large and high-contrast, max 3–4 words.
- [ ] Music and visuals: use only royalty-free or properly licensed assets.

## 2. Language plan (English, Hindi, Assamese)

| Option | How | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended if available)** | One upload per topic, English as the main audio, Hindi and Assamese added as **extra audio tracks** (YouTube Studio → Languages → Add audio). Add translated titles and descriptions in the same screen. | Keeping views, likes and comments on one video; least work |
| **B** | Separate Hindi and Assamese channels, each uploading its own language version with its own chapter timings | Regional discovery (Assam, Hindi belt) if you'll run multiple channels |

⚠ Multi-language audio isn't available on every channel or for Shorts. Check your YouTube Studio before deciding. Don't upload the same video three times on one channel in three languages; it splits your audience and can look like duplicate content.

**Reels / Shorts:** post the English reel as a YouTube Short (linked to the long video, see section 6). Use the Hindi and Assamese reels on Instagram (or on the language channels if you choose option B).

## 3. Upload schedule (10 weeks)

One long video a week, plus its reel a few days later. The reel links back to the long video, so each reel feeds the next week's audience too.

| Week | Long video (e.g. Saturday) | Reel (e.g. Tuesday after) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 01 Unfinished tasks | 01 reel |
| 2 | 02 Procrastination | 02 reel |
| 3 | 03 Monday plans | 03 reel |
| 4 | 04 Multitasking | 04 reel |
| 5 | 05 Silent phone | 05 reel |
| 6 | 06 Tomato timer | 06 reel |
| 7 | 07 Four hours | 07 reel |
| 8 | 08 Boredom | 08 reel |
| 9 | 09 Productivity hacks | 09 reel |
| 10 | 10 Focus hijacked (finale) | 10 reel |

- Keep the same day and time every week. After 3–4 uploads, check **YouTube Analytics → Audience → When your viewers are on YouTube** and move the slot to match.
- Each outro teases the next video, so **don't change the order**.
- Optional: release reels 01–03 in the week before launch as teasers. Use "full video Saturday" in the caption and update the related-video link after the long video is live.
