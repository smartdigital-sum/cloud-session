# 08 · Reel: Shock or Boredom?

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~110 English (calm mentor pace, ~140 wpm, with pauses)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Bare white room; a finger hovers over a red button | **SHOCK > BOREDOM?** ⚡ | People chose electric shocks over sitting quietly with their thoughts. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:13 | Wall clock ticks 15 minutes; button pressed; small cartoon zap | 15 MIN ALONE · 2014 | In a 2014 study, many people left alone for fifteen minutes shocked themselves. Even some who'd said they'd pay to avoid it. |
| 3 | 0:13–0:23 | Hand copies phone-book numbers, eyes glazing; then idea bubbles pop from two plastic cups | BORED → MORE IDEAS | But boredom may be useful. People who'd copied phone numbers came up with more ideas for uses of plastic cups. |
| 4 | 0:23–0:33 | Three break rooms: hard task, rest, easy task; the easy-task bar rises highest | MIND-WANDERING BREAK WINS | And in another study, an easy, mind-wandering break boosted creative problem solving more than rest or a hard task. |
| 5 | 0:33–0:41 | Head under a shower; slow gears turn, a lightbulb blinks on | SHOWER IDEAS ARE REAL 🚿 | That's why ideas arrive in the shower. A wandering mind keeps working. |
| 6 | 0:41–0:51 | Queue without a phone, walk without earphones, a person gazing out of a window | BRING BACK THE GAPS | So bring back small gaps. Queue without your phone. Walk without a podcast. Let your mind drift. |
| 7 | 0:51–0:57 | Three icons 🚿 🚶 🚌 with a question mark | WHERE DO YOUR IDEAS COME FROM? 👇 | Where do your best ideas come from? Tell me below. |

**Loop:** scene 7's empty moment cuts straight back to the empty white room in scene 1.
