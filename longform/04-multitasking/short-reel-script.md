# 04 · Reel: You Can't Multitask

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | Phone, snack, laptop; an ATTENTION spotlight jumps between them | **YOU CAN'T MULTITASK** | You can't multitask. Not really. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:12 | Track switch inside a brain: TASK A train stops, points clunk, TASK B starts; stopwatch ticks "+0.x s" | YOUR BRAIN SWITCHES | For anything that needs thinking, your brain doesn't do two things at once. It switches. And every switch costs time. |
| 3 | 0:12–0:23 | Clown on a unicycle crosses a campus square; a walker on a call walks straight past | 1 IN 4 NOTICED 🤡 | In one study, a clown rode a unicycle across a campus. Of people talking on their phones, only about one in four noticed. |
| 4 | 0:23–0:33 | Simulator screen; slow reaction bar for the phone driver; earpiece icon ≈ wine glass "0.08% US LIMIT" | HANDS-FREE ≠ BRAIN-FREE | In a driving simulator, phone talkers, even hands-free, were as impaired as drunk drivers at the US legal limit. |
| 5 | 0:33–0:43 | 200 tiny drivers flash red; 5 glow gold with capes | SUPERTASKERS ≈ 2.5% | Only about two and a half percent of people tested could do both without slipping. "Supertaskers." The rest of us? No. |
| 6 | 0:43–0:51 | Cards flip ONE THING · BATCH · OUT OF REACH; the phone slides into a drawer | ONE THING AT A TIME | So do one thing at a time. Batch your messages. Put the phone out of reach. |
| 7 | 0:51–0:58 | Browser bar with a row of tiny tabs; cursor hovers | HOW MANY TABS? 👀 | How many tabs do you have open right now? Be honest. Tell me below. |
