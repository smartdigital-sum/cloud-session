[whispers] Why won't your brain let go of the things you haven't done?
[curious] Berlin, the 1920s. A waiter remembers every unpaid order, with no notepad. The moment the bill is paid… he forgets it.
[calm] Psychologist Bluma Zeigarnik tested it. People remembered interrupted tasks far more often than finished ones. She published it in 1927.
[intrigued] But here's the twist. A 2025 review found that memory effect isn't reliable. What is? The urge to go back and finish.
[serious] And that urge costs you. In a 2011 study, an unfinished task kept pulling people's thoughts away, and made them worse at puzzles.
[warmly] The fix wasn't finishing it. It was a plan: what, when, where. Your brain just needs to trust it'll get done.
[friendly] So tonight, write tomorrow's list, and close the loop on paper. What's the one task stuck in your head? Tell me below.
