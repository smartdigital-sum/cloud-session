# 03 · Reel: Your Monday Plan Was Never Possible

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.

## Scene table

| # | Time | Visual (9:16) | On-screen text | Voiceover (English) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | A colourful planner page tears down the middle | **YOUR MONDAY PLAN WAS NEVER POSSIBLE** | Your Monday plan was never possible. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:12 | Top half MONDAY: bright figure, gym bag, ticked planner. Bottom half WEDNESDAY: same figure buried in papers | MONDAY ME vs WEDNESDAY ME | Monday you plans the gym, ten tasks, twenty pages. Wednesday you… what plan? That's not weakness. It's a bias. |
| 3 | 0:12–0:24 | Two calendar bars grow: GUESS 34 DAYS vs REAL 56 DAYS | THE PLANNING FALLACY · _Kahneman & Tversky_ | Kahneman and Tversky called it the planning fallacy. In one study, students expected their thesis to take thirty-four days. On average, it took fifty-six. |
| 4 | 0:24–0:33 | Sydney Opera House sails build slowly; calendar flips; price tag balloons A$7M → A$102M | 4 YEARS → 14 YEARS | Even experts fall for it. The Sydney Opera House was planned for four years. It took fourteen, at about fourteen times the cost. |
| 5 | 0:33–0:41 | Perfect zero-gap planner grid; interruptions fly in like birds and knock blocks out | NO ROOM FOR REAL LIFE | Why? We plan the work, not the interruptions. Monday's plan has no room for real life. |
| 6 | 0:41–0:51 | Cards flip 1 LOOK BACK · 2 BREAK IT DOWN · 3 LEAVE SPACE; new gappy planner absorbs the interruptions | PLAN FOR WEDNESDAY YOU | So plan for Wednesday you. Check how long it took last time. Break it into steps. Leave empty space. |
| 7 | 0:51–0:58 | The new planner on a desk; a pen hovers over a Monday list with ticks and blanks | LIST vs DONE? 👇 | How many things were on your Monday list… and how many are done? Tell me below. |

**Loop:** scene 7 ends on a planner page, which cuts straight into the planner tearing in scene 1.
