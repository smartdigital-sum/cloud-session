# 08 · Reel: Too Much, Too Fast

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start. Sensitive topic: gentle tone, no labelling of real people, helplines on screen.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was a question with nothing to wait for. | **Lock-in** teases "one test tells real love from a script". |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("so what do you watch for?", "online, it's often worse"). |
| Ended on a question only. | **Closing** = the test; **CTA** = full video + question that loops to scene 1. |
