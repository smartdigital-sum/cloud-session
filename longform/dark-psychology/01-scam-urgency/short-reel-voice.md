## English

**Style instructions**
```
You are an intriguing, authoritative, protective narrator for a 60-second awareness reel about why scammers make you hurry. Speak directly to one listener ("you"), like a trusted older sibling. Indian English accent. About 135 words per minute, with short pauses. Urgent on the quoted scam line, then calm and firm. Serious on scam types, reassuring on official facts, warm and empowering on the defence. Say the helpline digits clearly. End caring and friendly. Never mocking victims.
```

**Text**
```
[urgently] "Account blocked in ten minutes!" [short pause] That panic is the scam.
[curious] Almost every scam is in a hurry. Fear and urgency switch off the part of you that checks.
[serious] Fake KYC updates. Drugs in a "courier". And "digital arrest" video calls from fake police.
[firmly] The facts: police, CBI and ED don't arrest people over video calls. Banks never ask for your OTP.
[knowingly] And they'll say, "Don't tell anyone." Isolation stops others from helping you think.
[warmly] Your defence: hang up, call your bank's official number, tell someone. Cheated? Report on one-nine-three-zero, or cybercrime dot gov dot in.
[friendly] Share this with someone who needs it. What's the strangest scam message you've got?
```
