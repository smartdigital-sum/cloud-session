# 01 · Reel: Hurry = Scam

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels, written with the *Short-Form Storytelling* guide: Hook → Lock-In → Body → Rehook → Body → Rehook → Body → Closing → CTA.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~150 English (~150 wpm, short pauses only)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start. Keep **1930 · cybercrime.gov.in** visible in scenes 6–7.

## What changed and why

| Problem before | Fix (guide rule) |
|---|---|
| Hook was strong but promised nothing. | **Lock-in** teases one rule that beats every version. |
| Scenes ran as a list. | Two **rehooks** ("so what's real?", "and watch for one sentence"). |
| Ended with share + question. | **Closing** = the rule + helplines; **CTA** = full video + share + question that loops to scene 1. |
