# Dark Psychology & Manipulation Tactics (2.1 Psychology)

Faceless narrated videos (3–4 min, 16:9), each with a ~60s reel, in English, Hindi and Assamese. Rules: [../CLAUDE.md](../CLAUDE.md) · Ideas: [topic-ideas.md](topic-ideas.md)

**Framed for awareness and self-protection only** (see the rule at the top of [topic-ideas.md](topic-ideas.md)). Every video explains a tactic so viewers can recognise and resist it, and every payoff is a defence. Videos 07–09 (gaslighting, love bombing, DARVO) never label real people, say plainly where research is limited, and show helplines on screen and in the pinned comment.

Same four files per video folder as the other series: `long-video-script.md`, `long-video-voice.md`, `short-reel-script.md`, `short-reel-voice.md`.

**Voice:** **Charon** for the whole series (the same channel voice as the other series). The style instructions ask for an intriguing, slightly mysterious, authoritative narrator who is protective, and gentler in 07–09.

**Helplines used (India):** cyber fraud **1930** / cybercrime.gov.in · emergency **112** · women's helpline **181** · Tele-MANAS (free mental-health support) **14416**. In voice files they are spelled digit by digit so the TTS reads them as phone numbers.
