**How to generate a 4-minute track**
1. Paste the Style instructions for the language once.
2. Try all 8 Text blocks together in one run first. Keep the blank lines between sections, since they give natural pauses.
3. If the voice drifts, speeds up or cuts off near the end, generate **one section per run** with the same voice and style, then join the clips (add ~0.5s silence between sections).
4. Name files like `dp10-seven-signs_EN_s1-hook.wav`.

If TTS mispronounces "Roozenbeek", write it as "Rose-en-bake". Helplines appear on screen only in this video (not spoken), so no digit spelling is needed.

```

[short pause] If you've heard all three this week, someone may be working on you.

[warmly] Defence: pause. Anything real will still be real in ten minutes.
[brisk] Sign two: small yeses keep growing. A tiny favour, then a bigger one, then bigger still.
[warmly] Defence: judge each request on its own. "If this were the first thing they asked, would I agree?"
[short pause] Speed and steps. Both are designed to stop you looking up.

[knowingly] Sign three: a huge request, then a "reasonable" one. The first was built to be refused, so the second feels like a compromise.
[warmly] Defence: forget the first ask. Judge the second alone.
[knowingly] Sign four: a gift, then a request, close together. A free sample, a favour, a "free prize" that needs a small fee.
[warmly] Defence: you can say "thank you" and still say "no". And never pay to receive something free.
[short pause] A no doesn't create a debt. Neither does a gift you didn't ask for.

[serious] Sign five: a title, a uniform or a logo you can't check. A "police officer" on a video call. A "bank head office" caller.
[warmly] Defence: verify through a second channel. Hang up, find the official number yourself, and call back.
[serious] Sign six: "everyone's doing it". Reviews, followers, a group full of people posting profits.

[gently] Sign seven is the quietest, and the most important: you start doubting yourself.

[warmly] And here's the twist: you're already harder to fool. In 2022, researchers led by Jon Roozenbeek found that short videos explaining manipulation techniques helped people spot them, even in a real test on YouTube. Psychologists call it "prebunking".

[long pause] Manipulation needs speed, silence and secrecy. You now have time, questions, and people.

[friendly] Tell me in the comments: which of these seven signs have you seen most often?
```
