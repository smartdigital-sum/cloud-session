# Topic ideas: 2.1 Psychology: Dark Psychology & Manipulation Tactics

**Framing rule for this whole series:** awareness and self-protection only. Every video explains how a tactic works so viewers can *recognise and resist* it, and every payoff is a defence. No "how to manipulate people" angles, no targeting real private individuals, and no tips that could be used to deceive.

Narration style (psychology): intriguing, slightly mysterious, authoritative; relatable everyday examples and "Have you ever noticed…" moments; cite real studies accurately and say so when a finding is debated.
