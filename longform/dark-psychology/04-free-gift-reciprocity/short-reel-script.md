# 04 · Reel: Free Isn't Free

Short version of [long-video-script.md](long-video-script.md) for YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical · **Length:** ~57s · **Words:** ~110 English (~135 wpm with pauses)
**Voiceover:** English, Hindi, Assamese → [short-reel-voice.md](short-reel-voice.md) (same voice as the series: Charon)

Rules: hook on screen + voice in the first 2 seconds, no intro or logo. One idea per scene. The last line asks a question, to get comments and loop viewers back to the start.

## Scene table

| 1 | 0:00–0:03 | A cold bottle with a bow; zoom in: the bow hides a fishing hook | **FREE DRINK = 2× YES** | A free drink just made you spend twice as much. |
| 2 | 0:03–0:12 | 1970s lab: a "student" returns with two Cokes; another room, empty hands | 1971 · ONE FREE COKE | In 1971, a student handed some people a free Coke. Later, he asked them to buy raffle tickets. |

| 4 | 0:22–0:31 | A glowing thread runs from a gift to a wrist | WE FEEL WE MUST REPAY | Why? We feel we must repay what we're given, even gifts we never asked for. |
| 5 | 0:31–0:40 | Quick cuts: a sample tray, free tea in a shop, "Pay ₹99 for your FREE gift" | FREE… WITH A CATCH | Watch for it: free samples, free tea at the shop, a "free prize" that needs a small fee. |
| 6 | 0:40–0:50 | The thread snaps; the figure smiles, says "Thank you", walks on | THANKS ≠ YES | Your defence: if a gift comes with a request, call it a tactic. You can say thanks, and still say no. |
| 7 | 0:50–0:57 | A pile of gift boxes, one with a hook | SNEAKIEST FREE OFFER? 👇 | What's the sneakiest "free" offer you've ever got? Tell me below. |
