**Style instructions**
```
You are a curious, playful Indian storyteller narrating a 40-second Instagram reel. Indian English accent. Speak like you're telling a fascinating secret to a friend, not like a news reader. Very expressive with big changes in pace and pitch: start fast and punchy, slow down before every reveal, light smile in the voice, end with a warm, friendly question. Brisk overall pace, about 160 words per minute.
```

**Text**
```

[whispers] Truth is, nobody knows for sure. [short pause] [curious] But there are three theories.
[mysterious] Theory one: World War Two. Fuel was scarce, and some trucks ran on kerosene, which catches fire easily. [short pause] "OK" meant… "On Kerosene"! [playful] Keep your distance!
[cheerful] Theory two: old Tata trucks carried an "OK" emblem. Painters simply wrote "Horn" and "Please" around it.
[serious] Theory three: old trucks had poor or no side mirrors. Drivers couldn't SEE you, so honk before overtaking. [laughs] "OK" just filled the gap.
[surprised] Bonus: Maharashtra reportedly BANNED it, because it encourages honking!
[warmly] So… [short pause] which theory do YOU believe? Comment below!
```

```
[serious] तीसरी थ्योरी: पुराने ट्रकों में साइड मिरर ठीक से नहीं होते थे। ड्राइवर पीछे देख नहीं पाता था, इसलिए ओवरटेक से पहले हॉर्न बजाओ। [laughs] "OK" बस बीच की जगह भरता था।

[warmly] तो… [short pause] आप कौन सी थ्योरी मानते हैं? कमेंट में बताइए!
```
