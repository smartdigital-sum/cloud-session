# Reel topic ideas: Assam + India "why" questions

Each topic is one question a viewer has wondered about, answered in 30–60s.
⚠ = facts to double-check before scripting.

## A. Assam-specific (strongest regional pull)
1. Why does Assam tea taste "malty" and strong, unlike Darjeeling? (Camellia sinensis var. assamica, low-altitude heat, humidity)
2. Why do birds "commit suicide" at Jatinga every autumn? (Fog + lights + wind disorient migrating birds; it is not suicide) ⚠ the phenomenon has declined
3. Why is Muga silk golden and found only in Assam? (Antheraea assamensis, som/soalu leaves, gets shinier with every wash)
4. Why does the Brahmaputra change course and swallow Majuli every year? (Braided river, huge sediment load, soft banks, 1950 earthquake raised riverbed)
5. Why does Assam flood even in years with less rain? (Upstream rain in Arunachal/Tibet, silted riverbed, embankment failure) ⚠ check 2026 reports
6. Why does Assam's clock feel "wrong"? Sunrise ~4:30 am, dark by 4:30 pm — the Chaibagan time / separate time zone debate
7. Why is Bhut Jolokia so hot, and how hot is it? (Scoville ~1 million, capsaicin explained) ⚠
8. Why are one-horned rhinos only in Kaziranga and not across India anymore? (habitat loss, poaching, the horn = keratin, not medicine)
9. Why is Digboi's refinery still running after 120+ years? ⚠ (one of the oldest operating refineries in the world)
10. Why do Assamese homes give a gamosa, and why the red border? (symbolism, handloom economics)
11. Why does Bihu fall in mid-April / mid-January / October — three Bihus tied to the farming cycle
12. Why is Mayong called the "black magic capital"? What's history vs myth (manuscripts, Ahom-era healers)
13. Why is Assam's "Sattriya" a classical dance while Bihu isn't?
14. Why does Guwahati have so many earthquakes? (Indian plate pushing into Eurasia, 1897 & 1950 quakes, seismic zone V)
15. Why is the Brahmaputra the only "male" river in India? (name meaning, mythology vs geography)

## B. India-wide everyday "why" questions (big reach)
16. Why do Indian trains have yellow X marks on the last coach?
17. Why does the milestone colour change (yellow/green/black/orange)? Each tells you the road type
18. Why are Indian roads/trucks painted "Horn OK Please"? (origin theories)
19. Why do Indian toothpastes have colour marks on the tube bottom? (myth busting: it's a printing sensor mark, not ingredients)
20. Why do auto-rickshaws in different cities have different colours? (CNG vs petrol, state rules)
21. Why are Indian currency notes not made of paper? (cotton-rag blend, why they survive washing)
22. Why does India have one time zone despite being 3,000 km wide? (ties back to #6)
23. Why does mustard oil get banned/labelled "for external use only" in some countries? (erucic acid) — relevant: Assam cooks in mustard oil
24. Why does the monsoon arrive in Kerala first but in Assam earlier than Delhi?
25. Why do mosquitoes bite some people more than others? (CO2, body odour, blood type evidence) ⚠

## C. Hooks that work well in this format
- Start with the question on screen in the first 2 seconds
- Give a "wrong common belief" first, then the real answer
- End with a related question: "Next: why does…" → series viewing
- Add Assamese/Hindi subtitles for regional reach
