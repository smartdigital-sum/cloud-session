# Who first said "Jai Hind"?
Length: 40s | Format: 9:16 | ~95 words VO (English) | Reel type: **Pride / heritage**

## Research summary
- **Claim 1: Chempakaraman Pillai** (revolutionary, born in Thiruvananthapuram) is widely credited with coining "Jai Hind". One account dates it to **1907** ⚠ (date not firmly established).
- **Claim 2: Abid Hasan Safrani** (Zain-ul Abideen Hasan) of Hyderabad, secretary to Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose. He suggested "Jai Hind" as the slogan/salute of the **Indian National Army (INA)**. Some sources (e.g. Leonard Gordon, *Brothers Against the Raj*) say he coined it, as a short form of "Jai Hindustan ki".
- Historians are divided between the two.
- The INA used it as its salute/war cry; after independence it became a common national salutation.

Sources: Wikipedia (Jai Hind; Abid Hasan; Chempakaraman Pillai; Jai Hind postmark), The Hans India ("Jai Hind coined by a Hyderabadi"), The Wire.
