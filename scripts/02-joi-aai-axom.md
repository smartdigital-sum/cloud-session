# Joi Aai Axom — Who said it first?
Length: 40s | Format: 9:16 | ~105 words VO (English)

## Research summary
- Meaning: "Joi Aai Axom" = "Glory to Mother Assam".
- **Origin is disputed** — two poets are credited:
  - Lakshminath Bezbaroa (writer of the state song "O Mur Apunar Desh") — credited by Sentinel/folklorist Anil Saikia.
  - Ambikagiri Raichoudhury ("Asom Kesari") — The Telegraph says it is part of his poem ending "…Joi Aai Axom bol".
- Became the main slogan / war cry of the Assam Movement (1979–1985).
- Revived in the 2019–2020 protests in Assam, often with the gamosa.
- ⚠ Exact poem titles/years not confirmed — don't show a poem name or year on screen until verified.
- Tone: keep it neutral — about the phrase and its history, not about any party or side.

Sources: Telegraph (ttef.in) "Joi Aai Axom unites…", Deccan Herald "Gamosa, Joi Aai Asom…", Assam Tribune "Aah oi aah, ulai aah…", GuwahatiPlus, Wikipedia (Ambikagiri Raichoudhury).
