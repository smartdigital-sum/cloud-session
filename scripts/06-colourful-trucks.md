# Why are Indian trucks painted so colourfully?
Length: 40s | Format: 9:16 | ~106 words VO (English) | Reel type: **Mystery / origin**

## Research summary
- South Asian truck art is commonly traced to the **1940s**, when goods trucks first spread on the roads. ⚠ Loosely documented.
- **Punjab** is repeatedly named as a major hub; many truck painters come from there.
- Why so colourful (sources point to culture more than one practical reason):
  1. **Home on wheels:** drivers spend weeks on the road, so the truck is their workspace, home and place of worship.
  2. **Protection & faith:** gods, Om, protective eyes, evil-eye slogans.
  3. **Pride / standing out:** ⚠ widely said, but less directly sourced; keep the wording soft.
- **India & Pakistan** share the tradition: same roots, different styles.

Sources: Wikipedia (Truck art in South Asia), 91Trucks (two articles), The Wire, Dawn, Hyperallergic, Artzolo, The Citizen.
